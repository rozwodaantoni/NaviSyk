# NaviSyk
Privacy policy for a mobile app NaviSky
Last updated: May 31, 2026

What data we collect:
• Location data (GPS coordinates) — used to show your position on the map and record flights. Location data is stored only on your device.
• Flight routes and waypoints — stored locally on your device.

Third-party services:
• OpenAIP (map tiles and aviation data)
• CGI Federal AIM (NOTAM data)
• Open-Meteo (weather/METAR data)

These services may receive your approximate location to provide relevant data. Please refer to their respective privacy policies.

What we do NOT collect:
• We do not collect personal information, accounts, or emails
• We do not use analytics or tracking
• We do not sell or share any data

Data storage:
All user data is stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers or databases.
